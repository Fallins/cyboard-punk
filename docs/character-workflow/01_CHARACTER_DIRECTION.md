## Approved Stage 0 direction

### Identity and role

NYX remains the CYBOARD primary Operator / AI Signal Intelligence Operator. The redesign should present her as a **cold, elegant, premium command-center woman** rather than a robot, mascot, combatant, or generic cyberpunk character.

Her appeal is intentionally built around contrast:

- **work mode:** cool, focused, controlled, intelligent, professional, slightly intimidating;
- **direct user interaction:** warmer and more intimate, capable of playful, coquettish, sensual, teasing behavior without losing sophistication;
- she should feel emotionally present and human, not cold machinery.

### Visual archetype

Working archetype: **NYX — Cold-Elegant Command Queen**.

The core direction comes from the Stage 0 `NYX PRIME / Signal Director` concept, merged with the more refined fashion and silhouette language of the `AURELIA` concept.

The intended result is premium, mature and attractive rather than aggressive, combat-first, idol-like, or overtly mechanical.

### Face and realism

- adult, mature, beautiful woman;
- refined semi-realistic treatment;
- elegant facial structure with a cool, composed resting expression;
- eyes must support both focused work-state intensity and softer/flirtatious interaction states;
- beauty and emotional readability are identity-critical;
- avoid robotic facial construction, mask-like treatment, exaggerated anime/chibi proportions, or a generic stock cyberpunk face.

### Hair direction

Stage 1 may compare and refine these approved variants while preserving the same overall identity:

- long elegant waves;
- refined low ponytail;
- clean sophisticated updo / bun variant.

Hair should read as polished, feminine and expensive rather than tactical, punk, messy, or synthetic. Stage 1 must choose a coherent master hairstyle before reference lock.

### Body and silhouette

- visibly attractive, well-proportioned mature feminine body;
- elegant hourglass tendency with a defined waist and balanced bust/hips;
- long, graceful leg line;
- sexy without making the silhouette feel aggressive, exaggerated, vulgar, or combat-oriented;
- the target is **high-end sensuality / premium sex appeal**, not overt provocation;
- posture should communicate confidence, control and poise.

### Costume language

- premium clean cyberpunk / high-fashion operator language;
- structured, fitted tailoring rather than battle armor;
- may combine refined operator uniform cues with couture-like construction;
- dark luxury materials such as graphite, deep navy, matte black, smoked/translucent technical panels, restrained metallic structure;
- body-conscious fit is appropriate, but visual sophistication must dominate over exposure;
- costume should support both command-center authority and elegant femininity.

### Signature motif / CYBOARD identity

- retain a refined signal/core motif as part of NYX's identity;
- integrate CYBOARD's interrupted-C / diamond-node language where it can feel intentional rather than pasted on;
- signature lighting and signal behavior should reinforce the command-center role;
- the character must remain recognizable as CYBOARD-specific rather than interchangeable generic cyberpunk art.

### Palette

Primary:

- void / graphite / deep navy / matte black.

Emissive/accent:

- restrained cyan;
- violet / cool purple;
- selective magenta for reaction or intimate visual accents.

Glow must remain controlled and premium rather than nightclub-neon heavy.

### Medium direction

Preferred production direction after later validation:

- **2D / 2.5D** first, because facial beauty, expression fidelity and premium presentation are identity-critical.

Approved exploration media:

- 2D concept/reference generation;
- 2.5D reference/runtime experiments in later stages;
- 3D may be used as an exploration/reference/build technique if useful, but Stage 0 does not authorize a 3D runtime migration.

The eventual production choice remains subject to later workflow gates and Stage 8 Promotion Gate approval.

### Animation / behavior potential

The selected identity should support a strong mode contrast without requiring exaggerated gestures.

Work-state possibilities:

- focused gaze and provider attention;
- subtle head orientation and acknowledgement;
- calm breathing;
- restrained core/signal response;
- concentrated downward or lateral attention while processing.

Direct-interaction possibilities:

- softened eyes;
- restrained smile;
- playful eyebrow/eye behavior;
- small head tilt;
- elegant closer-attention pose;
- coquettish or teasing expressions/gestures that remain sophisticated rather than cartoonish.

Exact animation implementation belongs to later stages.

## Stage 1 refinement boundary

Stage 1 is allowed to refine and compare:

- the approved hair variants above;
- exact face features while staying within the mature beautiful semi-realistic archetype;
- exact body proportions inside the approved elegant/high-end sensual silhouette;
- costume construction, neckline, tailoring, panels and material balance;
- exact placement/form of the CYBOARD core motif;
- palette balance and presentation details.

Stage 1 is **not** allowed to change the core identity into a robot, mascot, combatant, idol, different age/archetype, different personality premise, or materially different silhouette/costume language without reopening Stage 0.

## Design-change policy after Stage 0

After Stage 0 PASS, changes fall into two categories:

### Refinement
Changes that preserve the selected identity. Allowed within later stages when scoped by state.

### Redesign
Changes to face archetype, core silhouette, costume language, signature motif, or overall identity. A redesign reopens Stage 0/1 and invalidates downstream visual gates as needed.

The agent must never hide a redesign inside a "small polish" task.
