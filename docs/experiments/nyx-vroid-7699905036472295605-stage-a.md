## Secondary motion and coordinates

`VRMC_springBone` metadata is present: 22 colliders, 10 collider groups and 49 spring chains
for hair, bust, hood, sleeves and coat/skirt parts. Generic `GLTFLoader` can load the geometry
without executing that physics metadata; an explicit VRM spring-bone runtime is required to
simulate it.

The untransformed root and bounds show a Y-up, ground-aligned character: feet are at y≈0 and
height is ≈1.64 units. The binary itself does not name a forward axis. Browser verification of
the isolated preview confirms its conventional +Z front view and +X profile view; the source
`Fcl_EYE_Close` target applied to all eight face mesh parts. Scale and centring are appropriate
for the production runtime and local validation surface without modifying the model.
